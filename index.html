How to play

The goal: move all of your pieces from your corner to the opposite corner.

  1. Step one squareMove a piece to any open neighboring square.
  2. Or chain your jumpsHop over an adjacent piece into the empty space beyond, then keep jumping.
  3. Fill the far cornerThe first player to occupy the opponent's starting corner wins.
